Sales Recording Flow (pos.php lines 182-275):

2.3 FUEL INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
------------------------------
Inventory Table: station_inventory
  Columns: id, station_id, product_id, stock_level, unit, reorder_level, 
           capacity, status, last_updated

Inventory Update Mechanism (pos.php, lines 254-256):
  Timing: IMMEDIATE (when transaction is saved)
  SQL: UPDATE station_inventory SET stock_level = stock_level - ? 
       WHERE product_id = ? AND station_id = ?
  
  Process:
    1. Validate stock BEFORE insertion (line 214-216)
    2. If insufficient: Reject transaction, stock unchanged
    3. If sufficient: Insert into sales → sale_items → deduct stock
    4. All within single database transaction (beginTransaction/commit)

Multi-Source Updates:
  ✓ POS Sales: Direct deduction from station_inventory
  ✓ Fuel Deliveries: Added via fuel_staff.php
  ✓ Fuel Readings: Deducted via fuel_shift_operations.php
  ✓ Adjustments: Manual add/deduct

2.10 CRITICAL GAPS
-------------------
Gap 1: No Pump/Nozzle Tracking
  - POS cannot tell which pump/nozzle was used
  - Cannot match POS sales to pump readings
  - Reconciliation must be done manually by comparing totals

Gap 2: No Calibration at POS
  - Sales recorded as-is without calibration
  - Calibration applied only during shift reconciliation
  - Potential for variance not captured at point of sale

Gap 3: No Real-Time Validation Against Pumps
  - POS doesn't check pump readings
  - Cannot prevent overselling based on physical limits
  - Relies entirely on station_inventory (which POS maintains)

Gap 4: Dual Inventory Systems
  - station_inventory (POS-driven, real-time)
  - fuel_inventory (Pump reading-driven, shift-end)
  - Not automatically reconciled
  - Can have conflicting "truth" values

Gap 5: No Transaction Linking
  - Sales records have NO FK to pumps/nozzles
  - No way to match POS sale back to pump activity
  - Reconciliation requires manual comparison of quantities

7. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The POS system is currently a STANDALONE SALES RECORDING SYSTEM that:

✓ Successfully records fuel transactions at the product level
✓ Manages inventory through immediate deduction
✓ Prevents overselling through pre-transaction validation
✓ Maintains complete audit trail

✗ Does NOT integrate with fuel pump readings
✗ Does NOT apply pump calibration to sales
✗ Does NOT track which pump/nozzle was used
✗ Does NOT validate against physical pump capabilities
✗ Does NOT automatically reconcile with pump readings

The architectural SEPARATION OF CONCERNS is intentional and functional,
but creates a GAP between recorded sales and physical fuel measurements
that must be reconciled manually or through end-of-shift procedures.

Future enhancement should focus on adding pump/nozzle tracking and
creating automated reconciliation between POS and pump reading systems.
